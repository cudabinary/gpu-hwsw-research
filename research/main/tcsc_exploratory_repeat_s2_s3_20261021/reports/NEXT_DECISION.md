# Decision after the finite ready-consumer repeat

The new prospective pairs consistently favor the prepared Direction consumer
over this pinned lean Push under the owned-output boundary. Retain this as a
reason to continue evaluating ready-consumer utility, not as a hardware result
or permission to start preparation experiments. The previous adverse S1 pair
and the unexplained cross-session Direction timing shift remain open evidence.

The next decision should first address whether the new regime survives a
separately approved, bounded boundary-matched replication with host-order and
setup effects recorded. If that interpretation is accepted, an independently
planned same-builder preparation-inclusive comparison becomes meaningful.
Do not start either campaign automatically. No new kernel, builder, tuning
campaign, input search, profiler or hardware mechanism is justified here.

A positive ready-consumer comparison cannot establish that our preparation
method caused the advantage: ordinary eager reverse construction and standard
Direction-Optimizing BFS can supply the same prepared state and consumer.
Reverse construction, transfer, allocation, memory footprint, synchronization,
unused-preparation waste and query amortization remain in the full-cost question.
Sequential preparation may legitimately win. No D0-D3 ranking is available.

The preserved evidence cannot identify SM issue, memory bandwidth/cache,
occupancy, queue or control bottlenecks. No new hardware need or generality
outside this small graph/contract has been established. MAIN remains a GPU
architecture investigation; neither TCSC disposal nor a final direction change
is made. RCC development and the independent WFA/FP4 papers were untouched.
